# BN Demand

**Mode:** Persuade (one-page marketing site)

## Product
BN Demand is a done-for-you organic LinkedIn demand system for executives, founders, and B2B teams (US, Canada, Australia). One program, $5,000 USD/month. No LinkedIn ads.

## Audience
Decision-makers who sell B2B and are tired of posting without pipeline.

## Proof (from wireframe — replace with assets when ready)

- +$35K/mo revenue — video production company, Melbourne
- Founder Chris: sales since 17, 1,000+ campaigns, 5,000+ people hired

## Primary CTAs
1. Book a 20-minute call (Calendly/Cal.com embed — placeholder until connected)
2. Free 10-point LinkedIn demand checklist (email capture — connect to ESP)
